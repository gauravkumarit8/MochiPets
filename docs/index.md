# Mochi Pets: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 November 2026

Mochi Pets ("the app") shows cute animated pets that walk around on your phone screen. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information. Developer: [YOUR NAME]. Contact: [YOUR EMAIL].

## What we collect

We do not collect, store or sell personal information. The app has no accounts and no sign-in. Your choices (selected pet, size, speed) and your pet progress (XP, level, happiness, daily reward streak, unlocked pets) are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes them.

## Permissions the app uses

- **Display over other apps:** used only to draw your pet on your screen. The app does not read, record or capture what is on your screen or inside other apps.
- **Foreground service and notifications:** a small notification is shown while your pet is on screen, so you can open the app or stop the pet.
- **Internet:** used only to load ads and the consent form described below.

## Ads

The app shows rewarded video ads using Google AdMob. Ads are shown only inside the app, and only when you choose to watch one to unlock a pet. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address (which can indicate your approximate location), app interactions such as taps and video views, and diagnostic information about app performance. This data is handled by Google under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [how Google uses data from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your Android settings (Settings, Google, Ads). Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app asks for your consent using Google's consent form, and you can change your choice later using the "Privacy and ad settings" button in the app.

## Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## Security

Data sent by the ads service is encrypted in transit.
